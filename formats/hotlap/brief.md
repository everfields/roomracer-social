# hotlap

- **Audience:** US sim racers and people about to buy their first rig.
- **Promise:** a real circuit, driven in RoomRacer's own 3D, in 15–20 seconds.
- **Hook:** the corner or the fact, on screen in the first second.
- **Look:** onboard and full screen, with RoomRacer's own timing screen. The car wears the livery of one of our rigs.
- **Sound:** an engine note made by code from the car's revs. No music, no voice.
- **Reason to follow:** every post is a different circuit, stretch or livery, and the site lets you drive it on your own rig.
- **Always:** the OpenStreetMap credit on screen. Only our own renders: every image not served by roomracer.com is refused while filming.
- **Never:** brand photos or logos, faces, names, our family story, claims of hands-on testing.

Direction to be approved by Carlos and Alberto (founder issue in `everfields/roomracer`). Until then it only runs dry.
